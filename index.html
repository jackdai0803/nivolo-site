Yours alone

No account. No cloud. No tracking.

Open Nivolo and start — there's no sign-up wall, because there's nothing to sign up for. Your goals, streaks, Pecks and gems live on your iPhone, not on our server.

The exceptions are the two optional AI features: drafting a plan sends the goal text you type, and the weekly insight sends that week's totals. Both are tied to a random ID, not to you. Nothing else leaves your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Before you download

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no password. Nivolo opens straight into the app and keeps your data on your iPhone.

What happens if I miss a day?

Usually nothing. You get a free streak freeze every week that's applied automatically, and you can schedule rest days in advance. If a streak does break, you can repair it from the home screen.

Is it really free?

Yes, and there are no ads. Every habit feature — unlimited goals, streaks, freezes, rest days, Pecks, gems, the widget — is free on Gaia. The paid tiers are optional: Zephyr doubles your Pecks and opens the whole icon set, Athena adds Nivo's AI planning and weekly insight, and Olympus shares Athena with up to six people.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Nivolo is iPhone-only for now.

What languages does it support?

English and Simplified Chinese (简体中文). You pick during setup and can switch any time in Settings.

Will I get nagged with notifications?

Only if you want them. Reminders are off-by-default in tone — one gentle daily nudge at a time you choose — and every category can be switched off in Settings.

What if I switch phones?

Your data is included in your standard iPhone backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings Nivolo with it.
