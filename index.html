Free habit tools. No account required.

Do I need an account?

No cloud account or password is required. The name and handle you choose make a local profile on your iPhone; they are not registered in a Nivolo user database.

What happens if I miss a day?

Usually nothing. You get a free streak freeze every week that's applied automatically, and you can schedule rest days in advance. If a streak does break, you can repair it from the home screen.

Is it really free?

Yes, and there are no ads. Every core habit feature — unlimited goals, streaks, freezes, rest days, Krill, gems, Season in Review and the widget — is free. Nivolo Plus adds AI planning and insights, faster collecting, the full icon set and a daily freeze.

What languages does it support?

Nivolo supports 14 languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Korean, Dutch, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Hindi, and Traditional Chinese. Pick yours during setup and switch any time in Settings.

More questions+
Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Nivolo is iPhone-only for now.

Will I get nagged with notifications?

Only if you want them. Reminders are off-by-default in tone — one gentle daily nudge at a time you choose — and every category can be switched off in Settings.

What if I switch phones?

Your data is included in your standard iPhone backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings Nivolo with it.
