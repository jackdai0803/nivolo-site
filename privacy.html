Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Nivolo is built to be private by default. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Your data stays on your device

Nivolo has no accounts and no sign-in. Your goals, actions, streaks, Pecks, gems, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data, and we run no analytics or advertising trackers.

AI-generated plans

When you use the AI plan feature, the goal text you type is sent to our server to generate your plan and is processed by an AI provider for that purpose only. It is not linked to your identity, and it is not used to build a profile of you.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They never pass through our servers, and we can't see them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details.

Backups

Because your data lives on your device, it is included in your standard iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), under Apple's backup terms.

Children

Nivolo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
